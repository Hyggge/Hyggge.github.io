Hi 👋 , I’m Zhenghao Chen (陈正昊), a third-year M.S. student at Beihang University and a Multimodal Algorithm Intern at ByteDance Seed, working on improving SeedRealtime. My research focuses on multimodal foundation models and agents, streaming video understanding, and spatial intelligence. Outside research, I enjoy photography 📷 , table tennis 🏓 , and city walks 🚶 .

My nickname, Hyggge, comes from the Danish word hygge. I love its spirit of finding warmth and joy in everyday life. I believe in “Less is More” and try to bring simplicity and focus to both my life and work. If you’re interested in my research or papers, I’d love to chat by email or over coffee in Haidian, Beijing.
